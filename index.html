Our Story

Born in the heart of Delaware, we're a small-batch pepper flake company obsessed with one thing: authentic heat that tells a story.

Every batch is hand-crafted using locally sourced ingredients and peppers grown right here in the First State. We believe in keeping it real—no artificial flavors, no shortcuts, just pure, unapologetic heat.

Made in Delaware. Felt Everywhere.

From our hands to your kitchen, we pour passion into every batch. Whether you're seasoning steaks, spicing up eggs, or crafting your own hot sauce, Delaware Pepper Co. brings the heat that honors tradition while pushing boundaries.
